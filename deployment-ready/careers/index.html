Coaching team

Join Our Coaching Team — Shape Future Champions!

P3 Sports is looking for passionate, qualified and dedicated sports coaches to join our growing team.

We work with schools, sports academies, residential societies and institutions to promote sports and fitness among children and adults.

If you love sports, enjoy working with children and want to build a career in sports coaching, we would love to hear from you!

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Conduct structured coaching sessions for beginners and advanced players.
  • Develop age-appropriate training programmes.
  • Focus on skill development, fitness and sports discipline.
  • Identify and nurture young sporting talent.
  • Monitor students' progress and performance.
  • Maintain a safe, positive and encouraging training environment.
  • Participate in school sports programmes, tournaments and P3 Sports events.

Who Can Apply?

  • Certified sports coaches and qualified trainers.
  • Experienced players interested in coaching.

  • Young sports professionals passionate about developing future champions.
