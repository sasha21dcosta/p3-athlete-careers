Scientific approach — LTAD

Our curriculum follows the long-term athlete development (LTAD) model, mapping age with skill progression so physical development and technique advance together.

  • Ages 2–7: gross motor skills — hand-eye coordination, walking, jumping, arm strength and fundamental movement (myelin development informs when complex skills are introduced).
  • Age 8+: progressive athletic and sport-specific skills that form the foundation for continued participation.
  • Scientific derivations guide how and when sporting techniques are taught.

Programmes

In-School Programs

Curriculum-based PE and sports sessions woven into the school timetable — structured programmes delivered during school hours with certified P3 coaches.

Summer camps

During vacations, P3 runs multi-sport summer camps on your premises — keeping students active and engaged. Schools may optionally open camps to outside participants for trust revenue generation.

Tournaments

P3 organises inter-school sports tournaments across partner schools — multi-day events from playoffs through finals, giving students a professional taste of competition in their chosen sport.

Sports tourism

For students ready to go further, P3 facilitates international coaching with federation-approved coaches in Europe, Asia, the Gulf and Australia.

Partnership model

Partnership model

Sports coaching in association with your school through two offerings:

During school hours

  • Delivered in scheduled PE periods across the academic calendar.
  • Target ~40 students enrolled per day across grades (from ~200 in PE) for impact and efficiency.
  • Senior dedicated coach per sport for the full academic year.
  • Sessions run for the full PE period; up to 20 students per session.
  • Pricing scales with enrolment — finalized with school management.

Post school hours

  • After-school coaching up to 3 days per week.
  • Minimum batch of 20 students; school-led enrolment encouragement.
  • Dedicated coach per sport for the academic calendar.
  • 1-hour sessions; up to 25 students per batch; at least 2 batches per day where required.
