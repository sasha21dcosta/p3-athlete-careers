Meet our founder

Founder — P3 Sports

Moonmoon Mukherjee

Moonmoon Mukherjee is a reputed table tennis player of international repute who has brought laurels to India and is a pride for the nation as the only Indian woman to become an ITTF Certified Level 3 Coach. With exceptional natural talent and sharp sports acumen, she has been a top performer nationally and internationally. Moonmoon took up the call to mentor and design the foundations of P3 Sports; under her guidance the company has grown to commendable levels of recognition, coaching and mentoring sportsmen while organizing corporate and social championships.

An ITTF Level-3 Coach and International Umpire, she has represented Bengal, UP and Indian Railways, officiated the girls' finals at the 2009 Youth Commonwealth Games, and won international and world championship medals in Greece, Spain, Vietnam, Turkey and New Zealand.

Leadership & advisors

The Director

Tushar Kanti Ghosh

An ace player of table tennis and a seasoned professional, Mr. Tushar Kanti Ghosh held West Bengal State Ranking No. 8 in 1971. He represented Burdwan and Calcutta University table tennis teams as Captain, served as Under Study table tennis coach for the West Bengal State Council of Sports, and holds the 1st Division Certificate. He presided as Founder General Secretary of Chandernagore District Table Tennis Association from 1972 to 1995, was Executive Committee Member for the stadium cell at the 1975 World TT Championships in Kolkata and for Press & Media during the 1985 World TT Championships in Delhi, and was member of the stadium cell at the 3rd SAF Games 1987. He is a member of West Bengal State Council of Sports and winner of the Best Organizer Award from Press Club & Government of West Bengal.
